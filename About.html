Our approachA more detailed look at the methodology

To rigorously describe the mechanism of our method, first I define some variables for you:

  1. CLF (Complete Logical Flow):
    The entire logical path that must be taken to understand a concept or learn a technique. It starts at one point and ends at another point.
  2. LG (Logical Chunks):
    The constituent parts of the complete logical flow. the entire flow is made up of a number of these units. The smallest, meticulously sized instructional units that introduce a single logical step. Designed to clarify the logic and prevent cognitive overload, these individual chunks seamlessly connect to construct the complete, low-friction logical flow of a mathematical concept
  3. CP (Connection Points):
    The critical nodes in the logical flow where one chunk logically flows into the next, guiding the student smoothly from stage to stage.
  4. CLI (Capacity of logic illumination):
    The ability of chosen instructional language to illuminate the underlying logic
  5. Prerequisites:
    Mastering new concepts naturally requires foundational knowledge. However, with our unique presentation method, you won’t need to endlessly review past topics. As our platform evolves, we aim to introduce adaptive learning—using system detection and your feedback to intelligently recommend foundational courses only when you truly need them.

In short, our methodology is built on three core steps:

  • First: Analyzing the CLF.
  • Second: Identifying CPs according to LGs minimization.
  • Finally: Optimize the instructional language to maximize CLI
